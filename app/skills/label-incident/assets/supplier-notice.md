# Supplier notice (label)

A factual notice to the supplier. It never blames and never speculates about how the defect happened.

1. **What is wrong.** For each finding: the rule, the field and the value, and which of the pack, the listing and the
   spec version disagree.
2. **Where it was found.** The lot when it could be read, the store that checked it, and when.
3. **What we have done.** The hold on the lot and the listing fix, as approved.
4. **What we need back.**
   - corrected artwork or label file;
   - every lot printed with the faulty label, and where those lots shipped;
   - the corrective action;
   - a date by which you will reply.
