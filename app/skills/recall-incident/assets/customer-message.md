# Customer message (recall)

Write for a customer reading on a phone:
- plain words and short sentences;
- no jargon. The business recalls the product; a regulator only publishes the notice, so never write that a
  regulator recalled anything;
- under 120 words;
- a subject under 60 characters that starts with "Recall:".

In this order:
1. **The product** and how to recognise the affected batch or best-before date, as printed on the pack.
2. **The hazard** in plain words, and what to do now, by hazard type:
   - **microbial, spoilage, foreign_matter:** do not eat it.
   - **allergen:** if you are allergic to or intolerant of the named allergen, do not eat it.
   - **chemical:** do not eat or use it, and keep it away from children.
   - **choking:** take it away from children now.
   - **electrical_fire:** stop using it and unplug it; do not try to repair it.
   - **injury:** stop using it, and keep it away from children when the notice says so.
3. **The refund:** return it to any CalmChaosRetail store for a full refund, no receipt needed, or keep it aside and contact
   us.
4. **Health:** anyone unwell or hurt after using it should see their doctor.

Rules:
- **Promise** a full refund and nothing else: no compensation, collection, timelines, statements that no one was
  harmed, or explanations of the cause.
- **Never include** order numbers, names, purchase dates or store ids. Code adds the order details to each message.
- **Follow the regulator's notice** for health advice. Never add a claim the notice does not make.
